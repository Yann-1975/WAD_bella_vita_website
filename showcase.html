Three examples demonstrating business thinking, customer focus and digital problem-solving.

01

Bella Vita Digital Transformation

Role

Web designer and developer.

A restaurant needed a modern online presence to improve customer engagement, communication and booking requests.

A five-page responsive website concept with a restaurant menu, contact details, social media integration, map directions and an email-based booking form.

Event & Customer Experience Planning

Planning and customer-experience contributor.

Design concept

Why the Problem-Solving Page is structured this way

The page begins with the business context so visitors understand the real-world problem before seeing the solution. It then presents the menu to communicate the restaurant's offer, social links to support engagement, contact details for trust and accessibility, and a booking/feedback form to create a clear customer action.

The red-and-white Mediterranean-inspired visual identity creates strong brand recognition, while animations add energy without distracting from usability.
